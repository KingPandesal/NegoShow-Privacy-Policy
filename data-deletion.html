User Rights & Privacy

User Data Deletion Instructions

NegoShow respects your privacy and provides users with full autonomy over their personal data. This guide outlines how you can submit a request to delete your account and all associated personal information.

Contents

1. Requesting Deletion 2. What to Include 3. What Happens After 4. Processing Time 5. Contact Information

1. Requesting Data Deletion

NegoShow respects your privacy and provides users with the option to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Direct Contact Email

If you would like to delete your NegoShow account and personal information, please send a data deletion request to:

negoshow.admin@gmail.com

2. What to Include

To help us identify your account, please include the following in your email:

  • Associated Email Address: The email address registered with your NegoShow account.
  • Full Name: Your complete name as registered on the profile.
  • Deletion Statement: A clear request stating that you want your NegoShow account and associated personal data deleted.
Facebook Login Note: If you signed in using Facebook Login, please use the email address associated with your NegoShow account when submitting the request.

3. What Happens After Your Request

After receiving your request, we will verify that the request belongs to the account owner and process the deletion of applicable personal data associated with your NegoShow account.

This data deletion may include:

Account information
Name and contact information
Business & entrepreneur profile info
Business preferences & selected categories
Target customer preferences
Preferred locations & budget details
Other personal information linked to your NegoShow account

Note: Some information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations or for legitimate academic, security, or administrative purposes.

4. Processing Time

We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period and may contact you if additional information is required to verify the request.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding account or data deletion, contact:

NegoShow Administration

Email: negoshow.admin@gmail.com
