Privacy Policy

NegoShow ("we," "our," or "the application") is a mobile-based business recommendation and store mapping system developed as an academic capstone project. NegoShow is designed to assist micro-entrepreneurs in identifying suitable business opportunities and understanding potential business locations and market conditions in Tagum City, Philippines.

This Privacy Policy explains how NegoShow collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use NegoShow, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password-related authentication information

Note: If you choose to sign in using Facebook or Google, we may receive information provided by the authentication provider, such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

Business and Preference Information

To provide business recommendations, NegoShow may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Entrepreneur or business profile information
  • Business categories or interests
  • Target customers
  • Preferred business locations
  • Estimated business budget
  • Other preferences used by the recommendation system

Business Performance Information

If you use NegoShow's business monitoring features, information related to your business performance may be collected, such as business status, performance indicators, and other information you voluntarily provide.

Location Information

NegoShow may use location-related information to provide store mapping, geographic analysis, and location-based business recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your NegoShow account
  • Authenticate users through supported sign-in methods
  • Generate business recommendations based on preferences
  • Provide location-based business & market information
  • Display businesses and stores on maps
  • Analyze business and market conditions
  • Provide business performance insights
  • Improve application functionality and usability
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application
Your information is used to provide the features and services of NegoShow and is not intended to be sold to third parties.

3. Authentication Services

NegoShow may use third-party authentication services, including Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, and Facebook Login. When you use a third-party sign-in method, the applicable provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

NegoShow only requests information necessary to create and maintain your account and provide the application's features.

4. Data Storage

NegoShow uses Firebase services to support authentication and data storage. Information associated with your NegoShow account may be stored in Firebase services and protected using appropriate authentication and access-control mechanisms.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Location Data

NegoShow may use location information to provide mapping and location-based recommendations. Location information may be used to:

  • Display your relevant geographic area
  • Identify nearby businesses or business opportunities
  • Support geographic and market analysis
  • Improve location-based recommendations

You may control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may limit certain NegoShow features.

6. Business Recommendations and Analytics

NegoShow provides recommendations and analytics based on available information and analytical models.

These recommendations are intended for decision-support and informational purposes only. NegoShow does not guarantee that a recommended business, location, or strategy will result in financial success. Users remain responsible for their own business decisions.

7. Sharing of Information

NegoShow does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or stored through third-party services used to operate the application, such as Firebase and authentication providers.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • Protect the security of the application
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Protect the rights and safety of users or others

8. Data Retention

We retain account and other voluntarily provided information for as long as necessary to provide NegoShow's services or fulfill legitimate operational and academic purposes. Information may be deleted or removed when it is no longer necessary, subject to technical, legal, or academic requirements.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Access information associated with your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information
  • Withdraw permissions for certain device features
  • Stop using NegoShow

To request assistance regarding your information, contact the NegoShow development team using the contact information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

NegoShow is intended for general users and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to NegoShow, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

11. Third-Party Services

NegoShow may use third-party services to provide application functionality, including:

Firebase Authentication
Firebase Cloud Firestore
Google Sign-In
Facebook Login
Mapping & Location Services

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to NegoShow, its features, or applicable requirements. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy will be updated.
